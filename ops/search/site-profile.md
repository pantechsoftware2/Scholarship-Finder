## Business Summary

Scholarship Finder is a study abroad scholarship matching tool for bachelor's, master's, and PhD applicants. It helps users shortlist scholarships based on academic profile, nationality, target destination, intended intake, major, English test profile, work experience, and profile highlights.

## Audience

- International students comparing study abroad scholarships.
- Students from India and nearby markets searching for overseas scholarships.
- Applicants considering the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, and other destination countries.
