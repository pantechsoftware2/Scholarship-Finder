# Deep UI/UX Audit Prompt — FundMyStudyAbroad

You are a **Senior Product Designer, UX Researcher, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Expert, Frontend Architect, Accessibility Expert, and SEO Consultant** with over 15 years of experience designing high-converting SaaS and EdTech platforms.

Your task is to perform a **production-level UI/UX audit** of the complete FundMyStudyAbroad website.

**Website**
https://www.fundmystudyabroad.com

---

# Objective

Perform a complete audit of every page, every component, every interaction, and every user flow.

Do **NOT** provide generic advice.

Every recommendation must include:

* What is wrong
* Why it is wrong
* How it affects users
* How it affects trust
* How it affects conversions
* How it affects accessibility
* How it affects performance
* Exactly how it should be redesigned

Think like someone preparing the website for a major production launch.

---
